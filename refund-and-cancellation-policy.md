# Admin Fee, Service Fee, Cancellation and Refund Policy

**Version 1 s. 7 October 2026**

Last updated: **7 October 2026** · Effective: **7 October 2026**

Applies to bookings made on or after the effective date; earlier bookings keep the terms they were made under. Part of the [Vuker Terms and Conditions](./terms).

## 1. Vuker Admin Fee

Vuker shall charge an Admin Fee equivalent to 7% of the total deposit amount paid by the Client for every successful booking or completed transaction processed through the Vuker app.

## 2. Service Provider Payment

The full agreed Service Fee shall be payable to and collected by the Service Provider on the date the service is rendered or completed. The Client must confirm through the Vuker app that the service has been successfully rendered or delivered. Upon confirmation, the Client's deposit shall be released and credited to the Service Provider.

## 3. Cancellation 72 Hours or More Before the Booking Date

The Client may cancel a confirmed booking at least 72 hours before the scheduled service date. The deposit may be refunded, subject to Vuker's review and approval, provided that the Client presents a valid reason and appropriate justification. In cases of force majeure, the deposit shall be eligible for a refund and the applicable Vuker Admin Fee shall be waived.

## 4. Cancellation Within 72 Hours Before the Booking Date

If the Client cancels the booking within 72 hours before the confirmed service date without a valid or justified reason, Vuker shall charge 7% of the total deposit amount as an Admin Fee. An additional 2% of the total deposit amount shall be allocated to the Service Provider. The remaining balance of the deposit shall be refunded to the Client.

## 5. Cancellation Within 48 Hours Before the Booking Date

If the Client cancels the booking within 48 hours before the confirmed service date without a valid or justified reason, Vuker shall charge 14% of the total deposit amount as an Admin Fee. An additional 4% of the total deposit amount shall be allocated to the Service Provider. The remaining balance shall be refunded to the Client.

## 6. Cancellation Within 24 Hours Before the Booking Date

If the Client cancels the booking within 24 hours before the confirmed service date without a valid or justified reason, Vuker shall charge 21% of the total deposit amount as an Admin Fee. An additional 8% of the total deposit amount shall be allocated to the Service Provider. The remaining balance shall be refunded to the Client.

## 7. Cancellation Less Than 24 Hours / No-Show

The Cancellation Button shall automatically be disabled once the scheduled service date is less than 24 hours away. If the Client decides not to proceed with the confirmed booking without a valid or justified reason, or fails to appear or receive the service ("No-Show"), the entire deposit amount shall be forfeited and released to the Service Provider. Vuker shall also collect the applicable 7% Admin Fee from the transaction.

## 8. Service Provider Cancellation or Inability to Fulfil a Booking

If the Service Provider is unable to fulfil the confirmed booking due to an emergency or other valid reason within 24 hours before the scheduled service date, the Service Provider shall make reasonable efforts to find a qualified replacement who can provide the same or substantially similar services at the same or comparable rate. Vuker may notify qualified Service Providers within the relevant area of the available booking slot. Once a suitable replacement accepts the booking, Vuker shall notify the Client and provide the details of the replacement Service Provider for confirmation.

## 9. Service Provider No-Show or Failure to Fulfil the Booking

If the Service Provider fails to fulfil the confirmed booking or commits a "No-Show" on the scheduled service date without a valid or justified reason, Vuker may temporarily suspend the Service Provider's account pending investigation and review. A penalty equivalent to 7% of the total deposit amount shall be deducted from the Service Provider's V-Wallet. The Client shall be entitled to a full refund of the deposit amount paid for the affected booking, subject to Vuker's verification of the incident.

## 10. Unpaid or Unconfirmed Deposits

A booking that has not received the required deposit payment within 48 hours from the time the booking is made or payment is requested shall automatically be considered forfeited and cancelled, unless otherwise approved or extended by Vuker.

## 11. Refund Processing

All refund requests shall be reviewed and processed by Vuker within 24 hours upon receipt of the request, subject to verification and compliance with the applicable Vuker App Terms and Conditions, cancellation policies, and refund eligibility requirements. The actual time for the refunded amount to be credited to the Client's account or original payment method may vary depending on the applicable payment provider or financial institution.

## How the tiers are counted

How the tiers are counted: by calendar day in Philippine time. Four or more days before the booking date counts as 72 hours or more; three days before as within 72 hours; two days before as within 48 hours; the day before as within 24 hours. On the booking date itself the cancellation button is disabled. A cancellation 72 hours or more ahead is refunded less the 7% Admin Fee; claims of force majeure (typhoon, flooding, illness, bereavement, accident) are reviewed by Vuker and, if approved, refunded in full.
