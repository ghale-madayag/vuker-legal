## 2. What we collect, and why

We collect only what a booking or a verification actually needs. Grouped by
what it is for, because that is what determines how long we keep it.

### 2.1 If you only browse

Nothing that identifies you. You can search Service Providers, view portfolios
and see prices without an account. Your approximate location is used **only on
your device** to sort results by distance, and is not stored on our servers.
Signed out, you cannot see any Service Provider's exact coordinates — the server
returns a distance already rounded, not a position.

### 2.2 If you create an account

| Data | Why | Basis under RA 10173 |
|---|---|---|
| Name, email | To identify you to the other party in a booking | Contract |
| Password (hashed) or Google sign-in identifier | To let you sign in | Contract |
| Mobile number, if you add one | Booking notifications by SMS | Consent |
| City and approximate location, if you allow it | To match you with nearby Service Providers | Consent |
| Whether you are a Client or a Service Provider | Determines what you can do | Contract |
| Service preferences, if the app records them — such as the categories you browse or book | To show you relevant Service Providers | Legitimate interest |

We never see your Google password. If you sign in with Google we receive only
your name, email address and profile picture.

### 2.3 If you are a Service Provider seeking verification

This is the most sensitive data we handle, and it is treated separately.

Vuker may verify Service Providers at different levels — basic profile, identity,
business and professional — as described in the Terms. What we ask for depends on
the level and on your trade.

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Full legal name, ID type, ID expiry date, **last four digits only** of the ID number | To check the ID is real and current (identity verification) |
| Photographs of your government ID (front, and back where the ID has one) | So a reviewer can confirm the document and the name |
| A selfie | To confirm the person holding the phone is the person on the ID |
| Business registration or permit, if you provide one | For business verification — optional |
| Professional licenses, certifications, permits or other credentials your trade requires | For professional verification, where the service legally requires authorization |
| For photography and video, three original camera files | To confirm the portfolio is your work. We read the camera make, model and capture date from the file's own metadata |

**We never store your full ID number.** Only the last four digits are written
to the database.

Government IDs and biometric comparison are **sensitive personal information**
under RA 10173 §3(l). We process them only with your explicit, separate consent
— the tick box on the verification screen — and you cannot submit without it.

A verification badge records what we checked. It is not a guarantee of a
Service Provider's future conduct, service quality or legal compliance.

### 2.4 If you pay a deposit

Card details **never reach Vuker**. When you save a card, the number, expiry and
CVC go from your device directly to **PayMongo**, our payment processor, which
returns a token. What we store is:

- the token (a reference that cannot be turned back into a card number);
- the card brand and the **last four digits**, so you can tell your cards apart;
- for e-wallets, the wallet type only.

We hold **no** full card numbers, no CVCs and no expiry dates. This is
deliberate: it keeps your card data out of our systems entirely.

We also keep a record of each payment — amount, currency, method type, status
and PayMongo's reference, and any Vuker fee charged — because we are required
to account for money taken. If a payment fails, is reversed or is disputed, we
keep the record of that too.

### 2.5 Messages and bookings

Messages between you and the other party, and the details of each booking
(service, date, time, location, package, price, deposit, cancellation and
refund terms, and status). Both parties to a booking can see it.

**Vuker may also access them**, but only when there is a reason to: to resolve a
dispute between a Client and a Service Provider, to answer a support request, to
investigate suspected fraud or misuse, or to keep the platform secure. In a
dispute we may ask either party for messages, receipts, payment records, photos,
documents or other evidence, and we use what is provided only for that dispute.

### 2.6 What a Service Provider's profile shows

If you are a Service Provider, your profile is public: it is shown to Clients and
to anyone browsing, signed in or not. It shows your name or business name, your
trade, your city or service area, your rates, your availability, your portfolio,
your verification level, your reviews and ratings, and — if you set one — your
office or shop address and hours, so Clients know where to meet you. Only put on
your profile what you are happy to have seen.

### 2.7 Reviews and ratings

If you are a Client, you may review and rate Service Providers based on your genuine experience with them.
Your review and rating are shown on that Service Provider's profile, to other
users. We may remove or restrict reviews that break the Terms — for example
fake, threatening or abusive reviews, or ones that post someone's personal
information — and restrict accounts involved in manipulating reviews.

### 2.8 What we use it for

Beyond the purposes in each section above, we use personal information to:
create and run accounts; verify identities and Service Providers; manage
bookings; process payments; communicate with you; provide customer support;
prevent fraud; keep the platform secure; resolve disputes; improve the service;
and meet our legal obligations. We will tell you before using it for anything
else.

## 3. How long we keep it

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| **ID photographs and your selfie** | **Deleted 30 days after the verification decision.** Only the outcome — verified or not, and at what level — is kept |
| Licenses, certifications, permits and business documents | While they support your verification, and afterwards only for as long as needed to resolve a dispute or meet a legal obligation |
| Verification outcome and audit trail | While your account exists, and afterwards only for as long as needed to resolve a dispute or meet a legal obligation |
| Account details | While your account exists |
| A Service Provider's public profile | While the account exists; it is taken down when the account is deleted |
| Bookings, payment records and invoices | **[10]** years after the booking, to meet BIR record-keeping requirements — confirm this figure with your accountant |
| Messages | While your account exists, and longer only where they are needed for an open dispute or legal claim |
| Reviews and ratings | As long as reasonably necessary for the purpose they were collected for, and for any dispute, security matter or legal claim |
| Approximate location | Not stored on our servers |

As the Data Privacy Act requires, we do not keep personal information longer than
its purpose needs, except where the law or a dispute, security matter or legal
claim requires it.

Deleting your account withdraws your verification consent and removes your
profile. Records we are legally required to retain — chiefly financial ones —
survive deletion, and only for as long as that requirement lasts. See
[Deleting your account and data](./data-deletion).

## 4. Who else sees your data

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising. We
share it only with the following, and only for the purpose named.

| Who | What they get | Where they process it |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** — database, authentication and file storage | Everything above | **Sydney, Australia** (`ap-southeast-2`) |
| **PayMongo** — payments | Card and wallet details you enter at payment, and the deposit amount | Philippines |
| **Semaphore** — SMS | Your mobile number and the message | Philippines |
| **Google** — if you use Google sign-in | Only what is needed to authenticate you | Global |
| The other party to your booking | Your name, and the booking details | — |
| Anyone browsing Vuker | A Service Provider's public profile (§2.6) and published reviews (§2.7) | — |

We share with the other party to a booking only what the booking needs. If we
add another provider — for example for maps, analytics or notifications — we
will add it to this table, with what it receives and where, before it starts
processing your data. We may also disclose information where the law requires
it.

**On sending your data abroad.** Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure in **Sydney, Australia**, not in the Philippines. RA 10173 holds us responsible for it wherever it is processed, and we remain accountable for it there. We rejected one face-matching
provider specifically because it would have sent Philippine government IDs to
servers in China, which we judged an unreasonable cross-border transfer for
this purpose. If we adopt automated face matching we intend to use a provider
processing within **ap-southeast-1 (Singapore)** or the Philippines, and we
will update this notice **before** doing so.

Automated face matching is **not currently enabled**. Verification is decided
by a human reviewer.

**Messages from us.** We send messages needed to run your account and bookings —
confirmations, reminders, payment notices, cancellations, security alerts and
changes to our policies. We may also send you promotional messages about Vuker,
with your consent where the law requires it, and every one lets you opt out.
Opting out does not stop the messages your bookings need.

## 5. Automated decisions

We do not make solely automated decisions that significantly affect you.
Verification is decided by a person. If face-matching software is enabled later,
it will produce a *score for the reviewer*, not a verdict — and you may ask for
the reasoning and contest the outcome.

## 6. Your rights

Under RA 10173 you may:

- **be informed** — this notice, and any change to it;
- **access** the data we hold about you;
- **object** to processing, and withdraw consent for anything based on it —
  including promotional messages, at any time;
- **correct** anything inaccurate;
- **erase or block** data processed unlawfully or no longer needed;
- **damages** for a violation that harms you;
- **data portability** — receive your data in a portable electronic format;
- **complain** to the National Privacy Commission.

To exercise any of these, contact our DPO at **asinobagojr2022@gmail.com**. We will respond
within **[15]** working days.

One limit worth stating plainly: whether you signed up as a Client or a
Service Provider **cannot be changed after your account is created**. It
determines what you are permitted to do throughout the service. If you need the
other role, create a separate account.

You may complain to the **National Privacy Commission**, 5th Floor, Delegation
Building, PICC Complex, Pasay City, or at privacy.gov.ph.

## 7. How we protect it

- Every request is authorised per-row in the database, so one account cannot
  read another's bookings, messages or documents.
- Verification documents live in **private storage**. They are reachable only
  through short-lived links issued to you or to a reviewer.
- Payment card data never enters our systems (§2.4).
- Deposits can only be marked paid by our payment server after the processor
  confirms the money moved — not by any app or device.
- Verification verdicts cannot be written from a device, only by review.
- Access by Vuker staff to messages and booking records (§2.5) is limited to
  the purposes stated there.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts you at risk we will notify you
and the NPC within **72 hours** of becoming aware, as RA 10173 requires.

**Your part.** Personal information you receive through Vuker — another user's
name, number or address — is for the booking it came with. The Terms prohibit
misusing, selling, publishing or passing it on.

believe a child has given us personal information, contact our DPO and we will
delete it.
